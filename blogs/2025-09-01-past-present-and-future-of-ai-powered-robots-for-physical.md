Large enterprises are rethinking physical security with intelligent automation. This session traces the evolution from early robotic process automation (RPA) to today’s AI and machine learning (ML)–powered robots—and looks ahead to what’s next. We’ll show how organizations are connecting cameras, access control, sensors, and mobile robotics to reduce false alarms, accelerate response, and lower total cost of ownership—without rip-and-replace.
